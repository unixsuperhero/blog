## Why am I writing about classes?

I'm writing about classes, because the ideas I want to share, much like a good
app, should be built on a solid foundation.  You might think fundamentals are
boring, maybe they are.  But that doesn't make them any less important.

Building better software requires more than just a surface level understanding
of the basics.  Much like kung fu, practicing basics should be a key element of
your craft.  At the very least, they should be revisited regularly.

it's ok to stray, it's not frowned upon or forbidden, but ideally it happens
after you have a firm grasp of the basics and are making the choice
deliberately with a real reason.
