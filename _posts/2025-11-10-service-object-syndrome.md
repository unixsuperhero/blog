date: 2025-11-10

I see a lot misconceptions about traditional "good practices" in software.

I've spent way too much time wondering why people don't understand the value
in certain principles, or worse, they outright dismiss them.

One common reason is procedural thinking.

If you want to be less-specific, we can say that missing context affects their
mental model.  In other words, a flawed mental model.

If two people are having a discussion, but don’t share the same context,
they’re not really having one conversation — they’re having two.

# What is procedural thinking?

Thinking about programming as defining sequences of steps.

When I say procedural thinking, I'm really using it as an umbrella term for 
a bunch of mistakes I see developers make.
